EN 1. English Privacy Policy

Scarlet Survivors is a completely offline, free-to-play, ad-free fan game. We do not collect, store on external servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal, usage, or device data.

  • No Network / Internet Permission: The Google Play release of Scarlet Survivors does not request Android’s INTERNET permission. It contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting telemetry, no advertising networks, and no external backend servers.
  • Zero Data Collection & Sharing: We do not collect names, email addresses, precise or approximate location, advertising IDs, device identifiers, diagnostics, or gameplay telemetry. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Local Save Storage Only: Your game progress (points, unlocked heroes, PowerUps, settings, and language preference) is saved strictly locally inside your device’s private app sandbox (eosd_save.json).
  • Data Deletion: Because all save data resides solely on your device, you can permanently delete it at any time using the in-game Reset button, clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the application.
  • Optional User-Initiated External Links: The title screen provides optional external buttons (such as X / Twitter and Google Play Rate 5★). Your device browser or the Google Play Store app is only opened when you explicitly tap those buttons; Scarlet Survivors itself never performs background network requests.
  • Children’s Privacy: Because the application collects no personal information or telemetry from any user, no data from children or adults is ever collected, processed, or stored.
  • Derivative Work Attribution: Scarlet Survivors is an unofficial, non-commercial fan-made derivative work inspired by Touhou Project (© Team Shanghai Alice / ZUN) and complies with Team Shanghai Alice’s public fan work guidelines.
  • Developer Contact: For privacy or support inquiries, reach the developer via remilya.dev, X (@Remilya356), or GitHub (@Remilya).

  • Yalnızca Cihaz İçi Kayıt: Oyun ilerlemeniz (puanlar, açılan karakterler, PowerUp yükseltmeleri, ayarlar ve dil tercihi) yalnızca cihazınızdaki özel uygulama alanında (eosd_save.json) saklanır.

JA 3. 日本語 プライバシーポリシー

Scarlet Survivors は完全オフライン動作・完全無料・広告なしの二次創作ファンゲームです。 個人データ、端末情報、解析データの収集・外部送信・販売・第三者提供は一切行いません。

  • 通信権限なし: 本アプリの Google Play 版は Android の INTERNET 権限を要求しません。アクセス解析ツール、クラッシュ収集 SDK、広告ネットワーク、外部サーバーとの通信機能は一切含まれていません。
  • データの非収集・非共有: 氏名、メールアドレス、位置情報、広告 ID、端末識別子、プレイログ等の情報は一切取得せず、端末外部へ送信されることはありません。
  • 端末内ローカル保存のみ: ゲームの進行状況（ポイント、アンロック要素、パワーアップ、各種設定、言語選択）は、お使いの端末内のアプリ専用領域（eosd_save.json）のみに保存されます。

  • お子様のプライバシー: 本アプリは年齢を問わず個人情報を一切収集しないため、お子様のデータが収集・保存されることもありません。
